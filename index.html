What is Tasbeeh Go?

Tasbeeh Go is a digital misbaha (electronic tasbeeh) that replaces a physical bead counter. It works as a dhikr counter, zikr counter, and dua counter, with adjustable targets, haptic feedback, and beautiful customizable bead themes. It is completely free, requires no account, stores your counts privately on your own device, and works offline as an installable progressive web app (PWA).

Features

  • Digital tasbeeh and dhikr counter with custom targets and laps
  • Curated library of authentic morning and evening adhkar and du'as
  • Build and save your own custom dhikr routines
  • The 99 Names of Allah with Arabic, transliteration, and meaning
  • Daily goals, streaks, a 12-week heatmap, and milestone tracking
  • Selectable Arabic typefaces including Naskh, Indo-Pak Nastaliq, and Kufi
  • Offline-first PWA — install it to your home screen on iOS and Android
